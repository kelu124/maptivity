## **INITIATIVE PORTFOLIO**

### **Initiative #1: Greenway Revitalization**

**Category:** Green Space & Environment  
**Scale:** Neighborhood  
**Lead Stakeholder Type:** Public-Private Partnership  
**Timeline:** Medium (2-3 years)  

**What it is:** This initiative focuses on enhancing and expanding the existing green spaces along the coasts of Blackwater Bay through the creation of a pedestrian-friendly greenway. A collaborative effort will involve local artists and community members to design attractive areas with native plantings, benches, and exercise stations.

**Why here:** Given King’s Landing’s vulnerability to flooding and rising sea levels, revitalizing green spaces will not only beautify the area but also mitigate environmental risks while improving community health and accessibility to nature.

**Who benefits most:** Residents of all ages living in and around the coastal neighborhoods.  

**Quick win or deep change:** Both  
**Estimated complexity:** Moderate  



### **Initiative #2: Artisan Market Festival**

**Category:** Arts, Culture & Heritage  
**Scale:** City-wide  
**Lead Stakeholder Type:** Community Group  
**Timeline:** Short (1 year)  

**What it is:** This initiative aims to establish a monthly artisan market festival celebrating King’s Landing’s diverse artistic heritage. Local artisans, musicians, and cultural groups will be invited to showcase their crafts, performances, and traditional foods, fostering cultural exchange and community pride.

**Why here:** King’s Landing’s cultural mosaic is one of its defining characteristics. By promoting local art and cultural expressions, this festival can bridge community tensions and reinforce bonds among different groups.

**Who benefits most:** Local artisans, cultural groups, and residents who seek diverse cultural experiences.  

**Quick win or deep change:** Quick win  
**Estimated complexity:** Simple  



### **Initiative #3: Housing Cooperative Project**

**Category:** Housing & Built Environment  
**Scale:** Neighborhood  
**Lead Stakeholder Type:** Non-profit  
**Timeline:** Medium (2-3 years)  

**What it is:** A housing cooperative will be established in lower-income areas like Flea Bottom to provide affordable housing options while promoting community engagement. This model allows residents to co-manage their living spaces, reducing rent costs and fostering unity among diverse tenants.

**Why here:** Given the stark disparities in living conditions within King’s Landing, this initiative addresses the urgent need for affordable housing while emphasizing social cohesion and community ownership.

**Who benefits most:** Low-income residents in Flea Bottom.  

**Quick win or deep change:** Deep change  
**Estimated complexity:** Complex  



### **Initiative #4: Digital Literacy Workshops**

**Category:** Education & Skills  
**Scale:** Neighborhood  
**Lead Stakeholder Type:** Academic Institution  
**Timeline:** Immediate (< 6 months)  

**What it is:** This initiative introduces digital literacy workshops aimed at equipping residents with essential technology skills for the modern job market. Partnering with local colleges, workshops will cater to different age groups, ensuring accessibility.

**Why here:** With King’s Landing's fast-evolving economy, residents need to stay competitive. Addressing digital gaps can bridge socioeconomic divides and empower vulnerable populations.

**Who benefits most:** Youth and working-age adults seeking employment opportunities.  

**Quick win or deep change:** Quick win  
**Estimated complexity:** Simple  



### **Initiative #5: Flood Resilience Hubs**

**Category:** Community Safety & Resilience  
**Scale:** District  
**Lead Stakeholder Type:** Government  
**Timeline:** Long (3+ years)  

**What it is:** This initiative will create designated resilience hubs in flood-prone areas to serve as resource centers during climate-related events. The hubs will provide emergency supplies, information, and support for effective community action during crises.

**Why here:** Given King’s Landing's vulnerabilities to flooding, the establishment of resilience hubs ensures preparedness and community solidarity in facing climate challenges.

**Who benefits most:** Residents of flood-prone districts, particularly those with limited resources.  

**Quick win or deep change:** Deep change  
**Estimated complexity:** Complex  



### **Initiative #6: Urban Green Transport Alliance**

**Category:** Mobility & Transportation  
**Scale:** City-wide  
**Lead Stakeholder Type:** Public-Private Partnership  
**Timeline:** Medium (2-3 years)  

**What it is:** This initiative seeks to expand on the “Horse and Cart Lane” by integrating more green transportation options, including electric carts and improved bike lanes. Collaborating with local businesses, the alliance will promote sustainable transport methods while reducing dependence on coal-powered carriages.

**Why here:** As a city embracing sustainability, this initiative aligns with the community's efforts to create environmentally friendly infrastructure while addressing mobility in densely populated areas.

**Who benefits most:** All residents who rely on transportation for daily activities.  

**Quick win or deep change:** Both  
**Estimated complexity:** Moderate  



### **Initiative #7: Community Food Co-op Initiative**

**Category:** Food Systems  
**Scale:** Neighborhood  
**Lead Stakeholder Type:** Non-profit  
**Timeline:** Short (1 year)  

**What it is:** A community food co-op will be established to offer affordable access to locally sourced food and promote sustainable practices, such as gardening workshops and nutrition education programs. This will empower residents to engage with their food sources and reduce dependency on impacted food systems.

**Why here:** With King’s Landing's reliance on surrounding lands for food, promoting a self-sufficient community-based food system can enhance food security and health among residents.

**Who benefits most:** Low and middle-income families with limited access to fresh, healthy food.  

**Quick win or deep change:** Both  
**Estimated complexity:** Simple
